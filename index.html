River Room

Game

Your home game with the boys: $10 buy-in, blinds 10¢/20¢ (the small blind adds 10¢ more to play), rebuys allowed. Chips: blue 10¢, red 20¢, green 50¢, black $1. Like the real game, people just call before the flop (a raise is rare and small), and a $1 bet is a big deal.

Games

Last player with chips wins. The players at this table each have their own style, and their styles change every tournament. Watch how they bet, figure out who they are, and use it against them.
